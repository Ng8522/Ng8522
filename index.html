Final Year Project – Enterprise Procurement Platform

An industry project provided by SeedNet Sdn. Bhd., developed using PHP Laravel, MySQL, and Tailwind CSS. The project was divided among four teams, and my team was responsible for the Automated Scoring Module. This module automatically calculates procurement proposal scores based on how well vendor responses fulfill customer requirements. Users can also manually overwrite scores after reviewing the submitted proposal documents. Additionally, the platform facilitates communication between customers and vendors to clarify details in proposals.

Staff Management Module - BookStore Management System

Used PHP Laravel in development. In this development process, the Facade design pattern is applied when it's required to give an easily understandable interface to some more complex system.
